# Pwani Lettings Tenancy Policy

Pwani Lettings manages furnished and unfurnished apartments and villas in Nyali and along the north coast.

## Deposit

The deposit is three months' rent for furnished properties and two months' rent for unfurnished properties. Deposits are refunded within 45 days of the end of the tenancy, after the inventory check and the final utility readings.

## Rent payment

Rent is due on the 1st of each month by bank transfer. Long-term tenants may pay quarterly in advance and receive a 3% discount.

## Late payment

A late fee of 10% of the monthly rent is charged if payment has not cleared by the 7th of the month.

## Notice period

The notice period is three months for furnished properties and two months for unfurnished properties, given in writing to lettings@pwanilettings.example.

## Pets

Pets are allowed only with the written approval of the landlord. Approval is given per pet and may require professional cleaning at the end of the tenancy.

## Holiday lets

Furnished villas can also be rented as holiday lets for a minimum stay of seven nights. Holiday lets are paid in full at the time of booking and include weekly cleaning.

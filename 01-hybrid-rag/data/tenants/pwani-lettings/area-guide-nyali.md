# Area Guide: Nyali

Nyali is an upmarket residential area on the mainland north of Mombasa island, popular with families, retirees and expatriates. Most Pwani Lettings properties in Nyali are within a ten-minute drive of the beach.

## Beaches

Public beach access points are spread along the coastline, and the beaches are busiest on weekends and public holidays. Swimming is safest at high tide, because the reef makes some areas shallow and rocky at low tide.

## Getting around

Tuk-tuks and boda bodas are common for short trips within Nyali. Getting to Mombasa island means crossing the Nyali Bridge, which can be congested at rush hour, so a 15-minute trip can take 45 minutes between 7am and 9am. Ride-hailing services operate throughout the area.

## Schools and healthcare

Nyali has several private international and local schools, as well as a large private hospital and many clinics, which makes it a common choice for families.

## Shopping and dining

Two shopping centres provide supermarkets, banks and restaurants. Seafood restaurants and cafés are found along the beach road.

## Climate

The climate is hot and humid all year. Air conditioning and good ventilation make a big difference, especially between December and April.

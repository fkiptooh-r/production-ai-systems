# Pwani Lettings Maintenance

## How to report

Email maintenance@pwanilettings.example with your property name, a description of the problem and photos. Requests are acknowledged within one working day.

## Emergencies

For emergencies such as flooding, gas smells, power failures or security problems, call +254 722 000 309. A technician will attend within six hours.

## Air conditioning

Air conditioning units are serviced every six months at no cost to the tenant. Report any unit that is leaking or not cooling, as salt air on the coast causes faster wear.

## Water

Properties use municipal water stored in rooftop tanks. During the dry months from January to March, water bowsers may be needed; the cost of bowser deliveries is shared equally between the units in a building.

## Pool and garden

Shared pools are cleaned twice a week and gardens are maintained weekly. These services are included in the service charge.

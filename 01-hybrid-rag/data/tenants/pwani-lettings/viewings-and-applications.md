# Viewings and Applications with Pwani Lettings

## Viewings

Viewings are by appointment only, Monday to Friday from 10am to 4pm. Book by emailing viewings@pwanilettings.example at least two days ahead. For applicants who live outside Mombasa, virtual viewings by video call are available on request.

## Applying

Applicants need a national ID or passport, an employment letter or proof of income, and, for foreign nationals, a valid work or residence permit.

## Holding fee

A refundable holding fee of KES 5,000 reserves a property for up to seven days while the application is processed. It is deducted from the first month's rent if the tenancy goes ahead, and refunded in full if the application is declined.

## Decision time

Applications are reviewed within five working days, because the landlord must approve every new tenant personally.

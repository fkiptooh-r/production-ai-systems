# Nyumba Homes Tenancy Policy

This policy applies to every property let through Nyumba Homes in Kilimani, Westlands and Lavington.

## Deposit

The security deposit is two months' rent, paid together with the first month's rent before keys are handed over. The deposit is held in a separate client account and is refunded within 30 days of moving out, after the final inspection. Deductions are only made for damage beyond normal wear and tear, unpaid rent or unpaid utility bills, and every deduction is itemised in writing.

## Rent payment

Rent is due on or before the 5th day of each month. Payment is accepted by bank transfer or M-Pesa paybill 400200, using the unit number as the account number. Cash is not accepted at the office.

## Late payment

If rent has not been received by the 10th of the month, a late fee of 5% of the monthly rent is added. Tenants who expect a delay should contact the accounts team before the 5th to agree a payment plan; agreed plans do not attract the late fee.

## Notice period

Either party must give two months' written notice to end a tenancy. Notice can be given by email to tenancies@nyumbahomes.example or by letter delivered to the office. A tenant who leaves without giving notice forfeits one month of the deposit.

## Pets

Cats are allowed in all apartments. Dogs are considered case by case and only in ground-floor units or houses with a garden. An additional pet deposit of KES 10,000 applies to every dog and is refunded with the main deposit.

## Subletting

Subletting, including short-stay or holiday letting of any part of the unit, is not permitted. Breach of this rule is grounds for ending the tenancy.

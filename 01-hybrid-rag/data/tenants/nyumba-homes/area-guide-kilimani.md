# Area Guide: Kilimani

Kilimani is a busy residential neighbourhood west of the city centre, popular with young professionals and families. Most Nyumba Homes properties here are apartment blocks of six to twelve floors built in the last ten years.

## Getting around

Matatus run along the main roads to the city centre throughout the day, and the trip takes 20 to 40 minutes depending on traffic. Ride-hailing is widely available and a ride to the central business district usually costs between KES 300 and KES 600. Morning traffic is heaviest between 7am and 9am, so many residents leave early or work flexible hours. Several streets have pavements suitable for walking to nearby shops, though some side roads do not.

## Schools

There are two international schools and several private primary schools within two kilometres of most of our Kilimani buildings. School buses serve the area and many apartment blocks have a dedicated pick-up point at the gate.

## Shopping and food

A large shopping mall with a supermarket, pharmacy and cinema is within walking distance of most units. Smaller convenience shops, cafés and restaurants line the main roads, and several supermarkets offer same-day delivery.

## Security

All Nyumba Homes buildings in Kilimani have 24-hour guards, CCTV at the entrances and controlled gate access. Residents register their visitors with the guard at the gate.

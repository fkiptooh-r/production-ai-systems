# Nyumba Homes Maintenance Guide

## Reporting a problem

Report maintenance issues through the tenant portal at portal.nyumbahomes.example. Each report gets a ticket number and you are notified by SMS when a technician is assigned. Please include photos where possible.

## Emergency repairs

Emergencies are burst pipes, total loss of power, a broken main door lock or any security risk. Call the 24-hour emergency line on +254 700 000 199. A technician will attend within four hours, day or night.

## Routine repairs

Non-urgent repairs such as dripping taps, faulty sockets or sticking windows are completed within three working days of the report.

## Water supply

Buildings receive municipal water on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays. Every Nyumba Homes building also has a borehole and a storage tank, so supply continues on the other days. Water is billed monthly based on each unit's meter.

## Electricity

Each unit has its own prepaid token meter. Tenants buy tokens directly from the power company; Nyumba Homes does not sell tokens.

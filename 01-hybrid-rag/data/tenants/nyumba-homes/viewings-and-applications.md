# Viewings and Applications at Nyumba Homes

## Booking a viewing

Viewings run Monday to Saturday, from 9am to 5pm. There are no viewings on Sundays or public holidays. Viewings must be booked at least 24 hours in advance on WhatsApp at +254 700 000 101, quoting the listing reference. An agent always accompanies the viewing; keys are never handed out for unaccompanied visits.

## Documents needed to apply

To apply for a unit you need a copy of your national ID or passport, bank statements or payslips for the last three months, and the contact details of a previous landlord. Company lets also need a letter from the employer on company letterhead.

## Application fee

A non-refundable application fee of KES 2,000 covers the background and reference checks. The fee is paid by M-Pesa when the application is submitted.

## Decision time

Applicants receive a decision within three working days. If approved, the unit is held for five days while the tenancy agreement is signed and the deposit is paid; after that it is released to the next applicant.

# Repairs and Utilities at Ruaka Rentals

## Reporting repairs

Every building has a resident caretaker. Report any repair directly to the caretaker in person or by calling +254 711 000 203. The caretaker logs the issue and arranges the repair.

## Emergencies

For emergencies such as flooding, electrical faults or a broken gate, call the caretaker immediately. Emergency repairs are attended to within two hours.

## Water

All Ruaka Rentals buildings have their own borehole, so water is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Water is included in the rent for bedsitters; one-bedroom and two-bedroom units pay a flat water charge of KES 600 per month.

## Electricity

Units have individual prepaid token meters. Tokens are bought through M-Pesa directly from the power company.

## Garbage collection

Garbage is collected every Monday and Thursday morning. Bags must be placed in the communal bins by the gate, not left in the corridors.

# Living in Ruaka

Ruaka is a fast-growing town on the northern edge of the city, known for affordable apartments and a lively high street. It suits students, young workers and small families looking for lower rents than the inner suburbs.

## Transport

Matatus to the city centre leave from the Ruaka stage every few minutes from 5am until late evening. The journey takes 45 to 75 minutes depending on traffic on the bypass. Boda bodas are the quickest way to move around inside Ruaka itself, and short trips cost between KES 50 and KES 100. Many residents working in Westlands use the bypass to avoid the city centre entirely.

## Schools and colleges

Several public and private primary and secondary schools are within Ruaka, and a number of university satellite campuses and colleges are a short matatu ride away, which makes the area popular with students.

## Shopping

Ruaka has two supermarkets, an open-air market on Tuesdays and Fridays, and many small shops selling groceries, hardware and household goods along the high street.

## Security

Ruaka Rentals buildings have a caretaker on site and a night guard. Gates are locked from 11pm to 5am; residents use their own gate key outside these hours.

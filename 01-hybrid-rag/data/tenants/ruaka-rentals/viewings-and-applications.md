# Viewing and Renting with Ruaka Rentals

## Viewings

You can view available units every day from 8am to 6pm, including Sundays from 2pm to 5pm. Walk-ins are welcome; ask for the caretaker at the gate and quote the house number from the advert. No appointment is needed.

## What you need to apply

Bring your national ID and one reference letter, either from a previous landlord or from your employer.

## Fees

There is no application fee at Ruaka Rentals. You only pay the deposit and the first month's rent when you sign.

## How fast you can move in

Applications are approved the same day if your documents are complete. You can move in as soon as the deposit and first month's rent are paid.

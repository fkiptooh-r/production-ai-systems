# Ruaka Rentals Tenancy Terms

These terms apply to all bedsitters, one-bedroom and two-bedroom units managed by Ruaka Rentals.

## Deposit

The deposit is one month's rent. It is refunded within 14 days after you move out, once the caretaker has checked the unit and confirmed that all utility bills are paid.

## Rent payment

Rent is due on the 1st of every month, with a grace period until the 5th. Pay by M-Pesa paybill 522100 using your house number as the account number. Payment receipts are sent by SMS.

## Late payment

From the 6th of the month, a late fee of KES 500 per day is charged until the rent is paid in full.

## Notice period

Tenants must give one month's notice before moving out. Notice is given in person at the caretaker's office or by SMS to +254 711 000 202.

## Pets

No pets are allowed in any Ruaka Rentals unit. This includes cats, dogs and birds.

## Subletting

Subletting is not allowed. Only the people named on the tenancy agreement may live in the unit.
